# Source, licence, and provenance record

## Code we derived from

**`src/bdh.py`** is derived from the official BDH reference implementation:

- Source: <https://github.com/pathwaycom/bdh>, file `bdh.py`
- Copyright 2025 Pathway Technology, Inc.
- Licence: MIT
- Retrieved: 2026-09-04

Retained from the reference essentially unchanged: `get_freqs`, the RoPE
rotation, the parameter set (`encoder`, `encoder_v`, `decoder`, `lm_head`), and
the token-parallel forward pass including the `tril(diagonal=-1)` causal mask
and the `x_sparse * y_sparse` gating.

Added by us, and marked as ours in the file docstring:

1. `forward_recurrent()` — a step-by-step formulation that materialises the
   synaptic state `rho` at every timestep, together with ablation hooks. The
   reference computes the same function in the token-parallel form and never
   builds `rho`. `tests/test_equivalence.py` asserts the two agree to ~2e-7.
2. `u_decay` — exposes the damping case of the `U` matrix of Definition 4 of the
   BDH paper. Default `1.0` reproduces the reference exactly.
3. `sigma()` — materialises the neuron-neuron matrix `D_y @ rho`.

**This is not an official Pathway model.** It is our own small training run of
the published BDH-GPU architecture on a synthetic task, at a scale far below any
reported BDH run. No BDH or BDH-CQ checkpoint was used, and nothing here
reproduces a published BDH or BDH-CQ result.

## What is live, precomputed, and synthetic

Declared in full in the README; summarised here.

- **Synthetic**: the MQAR dataset. Generated by `src/mqar.py` with fixed seeds;
  no external data, no scraped text, no licensing encumbrance.
- **Trained by us**: the model weights in `data/`. Our own run, minutes on one
  consumer GPU, fully reproducible from the committed seed.
- **Live in the artifact**: the forward pass and the effect of ablation.
- **Precomputed and shipped as JSON**: interference and decay sweeps.
- **Illustrative**: any smoothing or transition on the synapse view, labelled as
  such in the interface.
